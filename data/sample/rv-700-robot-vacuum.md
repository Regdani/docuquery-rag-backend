# RV-700 – CleanBot Robot Vacuum

## Safety
- Do not use the RV-700 outdoors or on wet floors.
- Remove cables, small objects and loose rugs from the floor before cleaning.
- Use only the supplied charging dock and power adapter.
- Do not use the robot on stairs without a barrier.

## Setup
1. Place the charging dock against a wall with 0.5 m free space on each side and 1.5 m in front.
2. Place the RV-700 on the dock and charge it for 4 hours before the first run.
3. Install the CleanBot app and scan the QR code under the dust bin to pair the robot with your Wi-Fi network (2.4 GHz only).

## Usage
- Press the center button once to start automatic cleaning.
- Press and hold the center button for 3 seconds to send the robot to the dock.
- The robot supports three suction levels: Quiet, Standard and Turbo.
- Set up no-go zones and cleaning schedules in the app.

## Maintenance
- Empty the dust bin after every run.
- Clean the main brush every 2 weeks.
- Replace the HEPA filter every 3 months.
- Replace the side brush every 6 months.

## Troubleshooting
| Code | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Err 3 | Main brush blocked | Remove the brush and cut away tangled hair |
| Err 5 | Cliff sensor dirty | Wipe the sensors on the underside with a dry cloth |
| Err 9 | Dust bin missing or not seated | Reinsert the dust bin |

## Technical data
- Suction power: 4000 Pa (Turbo)
- Battery: 5200 mAh, up to 150 minutes runtime in Standard mode
- Dust bin capacity: 0.45 L
- Dimensions (diameter x height): 34 x 9.5 cm
- Weight: 3.6 kg
- Noise level: 58 dB in Quiet mode
