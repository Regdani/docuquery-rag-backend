# AP-120 – PureAir Air Purifier

## Safety
- Do not cover the air inlet or the air outlet while the AP-120 is running.
- Do not use the purifier in rooms with high humidity such as bathrooms.
- Do not insert fingers or objects into the fan grille.
- Unplug the appliance before replacing the filter.

## Setup
1. Remove all packaging, including the plastic bag around the filter.
2. Insert the filter into the housing and close the rear cover until it clicks.
3. Place the AP-120 at least 30 cm away from walls and furniture, and plug it in.

## Usage
- Press the power button to switch the AP-120 on. The default mode is Auto.
- In Auto mode, the air quality ring shows blue for good, yellow for moderate and red for poor air.
- Press the Fan button to cycle through speeds 1 to 3. Press the Night button to dim the lights and use the quietest setting.
- The recommended room size is up to 40 m².

## Maintenance
- Vacuum the pre-filter every 2 weeks.
- Replace the combined HEPA and carbon filter every 6 months, or when the filter light turns red.
- After replacing the filter, hold the Filter button for 3 seconds to reset the counter.
- Wipe the air quality sensor with a dry cotton swab every 3 months.

## Troubleshooting
| Code | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| FL | Filter life ended | Replace the filter and reset the counter |
| E2 | Fan blocked | Unplug the purifier and remove the obstruction |
| E5 | Air quality sensor dirty | Clean the sensor |

## Technical data
- Clean air delivery rate (CADR): 320 m³/h
- Power: 45 W
- Noise level: 28 dB (Night) to 52 dB (speed 3)
- Dimensions (W x H x D): 26 x 55 x 26 cm
- Weight: 5.2 kg
