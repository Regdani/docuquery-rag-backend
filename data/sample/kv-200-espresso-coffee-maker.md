# KV-200 – Aroma Brew Espresso Coffee Maker

## Safety
- Use the KV-200 only indoors and only with cold, fresh drinking water.
- The steam wand and the brew head reach 130 °C. Do not touch them during use.
- Never immerse the base, the cord or the plug in water.
- Keep the appliance out of reach of children under 8 years.

## Setup
1. Place the KV-200 on a flat, heat-resistant surface with at least 10 cm free space on each side.
2. Fill the water tank (1.2 L) up to the MAX mark and insert it until it clicks.
3. Run a rinse cycle: press the Rinse button and let one full tank run through without coffee.

## Usage
1. Switch the appliance on with the power button. The display shows "HEAT" for about 40 seconds.
2. Insert the portafilter with 7 to 9 g of finely ground coffee.
3. To start the espresso program, press the cup symbol once for a single espresso (30 ml) or twice for a double espresso (60 ml).
4. For milk drinks, turn the steam knob and hold a milk jug under the steam wand.

## Maintenance
- Descale the KV-200 every 60 days, or after 200 brew cycles, whichever comes first.
- Descaling procedure: fill the tank with descaling solution mixed 1:3 with water, hold the Rinse button for 5 seconds to start the 25-minute descaling program, then rinse with two full tanks of fresh water.
- Empty the drip tray and the used-coffee container daily.
- Replace the water filter cartridge every 90 days.

## Troubleshooting
| Code | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| E01 | Water tank empty or not seated | Refill the tank and push it in until it clicks |
| E04 | Pump blocked by limescale | Run the descaling program |
| E07 | Overheating | Switch off and wait 30 minutes |

## Technical data
- Power: 1450 W
- Pump pressure: 15 bar
- Water tank capacity: 1.2 L
- Dimensions (W x H x D): 28 x 33 x 36 cm
- Weight: 5.8 kg
- Voltage: 230 V, 50 Hz
