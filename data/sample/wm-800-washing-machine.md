# WM-800 – AquaWash Front-Loading Washing Machine

## Safety
- Do not sit or stand on the WM-800 and do not lean on the door.
- Keep children and pets away from the drum. Check the drum before every wash.
- Do not wash items soaked in flammable substances such as petrol or white spirit.
- Disconnect the machine from the mains before cleaning or repairs.

## Setup
1. Remove the four transport bolts on the back before first use and keep them for later transport.
2. Level the machine with the adjustable feet so that it does not wobble.
3. Connect the water inlet hose to a cold water tap (pressure 0.1 to 1.0 MPa) and put the drain hose into a standpipe 60 to 100 cm high.

## Usage
1. Load the laundry up to a maximum of 8 kg for cotton. Do not overfill the drum.
2. Add detergent to the main wash compartment (II) and softener to the compartment marked with a flower.
3. Select a program with the dial and press Start. The Eco 40-60 program takes 3 hours 30 minutes.
4. Use the Delay button to postpone the start by up to 24 hours.

## Maintenance
- Clean the detergent drawer every month.
- Clean the drain pump filter every 3 months. It is behind the small flap at the bottom front.
- Run a hot 90 °C maintenance wash with an empty drum every 2 months.
- Leave the door slightly open after each wash to let the drum dry.

## Troubleshooting
| Code | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| E10 | No water inflow | Open the tap and check the inlet hose and filter |
| E21 | Drain pump blocked | Clean the drain pump filter |
| E30 | Door not closed | Close the door until it clicks |

## Technical data
- Capacity: 8 kg
- Maximum spin speed: 1400 rpm
- Power consumption: 2100 W maximum
- Energy class: B
- Dimensions (W x H x D): 60 x 85 x 56 cm
- Weight: 68 kg
