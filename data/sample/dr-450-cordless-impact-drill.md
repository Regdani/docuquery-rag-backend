# DR-450 – PowerGrip Cordless Impact Drill

## Safety
- Wear safety glasses and hearing protection when you use the impact mode.
- Clamp small workpieces. Never hold them in your hand.
- Remove the battery before changing bits or accessories.
- Do not use the DR-450 in damp conditions or near flammable liquids.

## Setup
1. Charge the 18 V battery pack fully before first use. A full charge takes 60 minutes.
2. Push the battery into the handle until it locks.
3. Open the chuck by turning the sleeve counterclockwise, insert the bit, and tighten the sleeve by hand.

## Usage
- Use the mode ring to select drilling, impact drilling or screwdriving.
- Gear 1 (up to 500 rpm) is for screwdriving and large bits. Gear 2 (up to 2000 rpm) is for drilling in wood and metal.
- Use impact mode only for masonry and concrete.
- The torque ring has 20 settings. Select a low setting for small screws.

## Maintenance
- Clean the ventilation slots every 3 months with a dry brush.
- Lubricate the chuck with a drop of machine oil every 6 months.
- Store the battery at 10 to 25 °C and charge it at least every 3 months.

## Troubleshooting
- Red light flashing quickly (code R1): the motor is overloaded. Release the trigger, reduce the pressure and let the drill cool for 5 minutes.
- Red light flashing slowly (code R2): the battery is too hot or too cold. Wait until it is between 0 and 45 °C.
- Green light flashing while charging: the charger detected a damaged battery cell. Stop charging and replace the battery.

## Technical data
- Voltage: 18 V lithium-ion, 4.0 Ah
- Maximum speed: 2000 rpm (gear 2)
- Rated motor power: 600 W
- Maximum torque: 60 Nm
- Maximum drilling diameter: 13 mm in steel, 25 mm in wood, 13 mm in concrete
- Dimensions (L x H x W): 21 x 24 x 8 cm
- Weight: 1.7 kg with battery
