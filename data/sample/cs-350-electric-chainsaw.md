# CS-350 – TimberPro Electric Chainsaw

## Safety
- Do not use the CS-350 in rain, in wet conditions or on wet wood. Electric shock is possible.
- Always wear a protective helmet with face shield, hearing protection, cut-resistant gloves, cut-resistant trousers and safety boots.
- Keep both hands on the handles and stand to the side of the cutting line.
- Never cut above shoulder height.
- Keep bystanders at least 5 m away.

## Setup
1. Mount the 35 cm guide bar and the chain, and tension the chain so that it can be lifted about 3 mm at the middle of the bar.
2. Fill the chain oil tank with biodegradable chain oil up to the marking.
3. Connect the saw to a 230 V outdoor extension cord of at least 1.5 mm² cross-section and a maximum length of 30 m.

## Usage
1. Press the safety lock, then squeeze the trigger. The chain starts within one second.
2. Let the chain reach full speed before it touches the wood.
3. Release the trigger to stop. The chain brake stops the chain in under 0.15 seconds.

## Maintenance
- Check the chain oil level before every use.
- Check the chain tension after the first 5 minutes of use and then every 30 minutes.
- Sharpen the chain every 10 hours of operation.
- Replace the chain after 100 hours of operation or when it is worn down to the wear mark.

## Troubleshooting
| Code | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | Chain oil tank empty | Refill the chain oil |
| C2 | Chain blocked | Release the trigger, unplug the saw and free the chain |
| C3 | Motor overheated | Wait 15 minutes before restarting |

## Technical data
- Power: 2000 W
- Chain speed: 13 m/s
- Guide bar length: 35 cm
- Chain oil tank capacity: 200 ml
- Dimensions (L x H x W): 48 x 24 x 22 cm
- Weight: 4.6 kg without bar and chain
