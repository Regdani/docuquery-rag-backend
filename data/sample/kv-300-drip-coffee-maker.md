# KV-300 – Aroma Brew Drip Coffee Maker

## Safety
- Use the KV-300 only indoors and only with cold drinking water.
- The glass jug and the hot plate become very hot. Use the handle only.
- Do not use the glass jug if it is cracked or chipped.
- Unplug the appliance before cleaning.

## Setup
1. Place the KV-300 on a flat surface close to a grounded socket.
2. Insert a size 4 paper filter into the filter basket.
3. Fill the water tank (1.5 L) with cold water up to the 12-cup mark.

## Usage
1. Add one level scoop (about 7 g) of ground coffee per cup to the filter.
2. Close the lid and press the Brew button. The brew light turns on.
3. When brewing is finished, the hot plate keeps the coffee warm for 40 minutes before it switches off automatically.
4. Use the timer button to schedule a brew up to 24 hours ahead.

## Maintenance
- Descale the KV-300 every 30 days, because it heats the water to boiling and builds up limescale faster than an espresso machine.
- Descaling procedure: fill the tank with a mixture of 50 percent white vinegar and 50 percent water, run a full brew cycle without coffee, then run two cycles with fresh water.
- Wash the glass jug and the filter basket after every use.

## Troubleshooting
| Code | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | Water tank empty | Refill the tank |
| F2 | Hot plate overheated | Unplug and let the appliance cool for 20 minutes |
| F3 | Brew time too long (limescale) | Descale the appliance |

## Technical data
- Power: 900 W
- Water tank capacity: 1.5 L (12 cups)
- Dimensions (W x H x D): 22 x 32 x 25 cm
- Weight: 2.4 kg
- Voltage: 230 V, 50 Hz
